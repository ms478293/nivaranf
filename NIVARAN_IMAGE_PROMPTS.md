## 🔷 LOGO REFERENCE (Include in every prompt as instructed)

The **Nivaran logo** consists of:
- An abstract rising human silhouette with arms raised in a warm **orange-to-coral gradient** (left side of logo)
- A cascade of small **star shapes** ascending from lower-left to upper-right beside the figure
- The word **"NIVARAN"** in bold, modern typography with a **teal-to-blue gradient** (cyan #68C9D0 → deep blue #3777BC)

When the prompt says **"Nivaran logo"**, place it naturally in the scene — on a badge, embroidered on clothing, printed on a bag, on a banner, or on a signboard — so it blends realistically with the environment.
